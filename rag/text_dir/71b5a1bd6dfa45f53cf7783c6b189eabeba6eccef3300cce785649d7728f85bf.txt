The Union Registry holds allowances for the execution of processes pertaining to the maintenance of the holding of accounts opened in Member States. (2) According to Directive 2003/87/EC, the Commission should designate a Central Administrator to operate and maintain the Union Registry, including its technical infrastructure. HAS DECIDED AS FOLLOWS: Article 1 The Director-General of the Directorate-General for Climate Action is designated as the Central Administrator of the Union Registry. Article 2 This Decision shall apply from the day of its adoption. Done at Brussels, 23 June 2025.
