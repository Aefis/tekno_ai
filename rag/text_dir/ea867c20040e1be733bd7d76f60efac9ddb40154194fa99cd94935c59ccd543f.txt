Following the Council Decision of 26 June 1975, adopting an indirect action programme for the management and storage of radioactive waste, the powers of the Advisory Committee on Programme Management, set up by the Council resolution of 19 November 1973, within the framework of the programme of direct action for the treatment and storage of radioactive waste, shall be extended to the administration of the programme for the management and storage of radioactive waste (indirect action). Member States will take this extension of powers into account when appointing members of the committee. 2. The committee will also be consulted on: - the detailed definition of projects, - the evaluation of work proposed, - the choice of bodies to which work is entrusted, - the review of the programme in accordance with Article 3 of the Council Decision of 26 June 1975.
