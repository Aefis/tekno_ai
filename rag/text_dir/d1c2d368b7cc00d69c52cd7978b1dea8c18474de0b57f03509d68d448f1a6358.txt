On 22 December 1999 it decided to extend the validity of the guidelines until 30 June 2000, except in respect of agricultural products. On 24 November 1999 the Commission adopted guidelines for state aid in the agricultural sector, which also apply to environmental aid in agriculture. (1) OJ C 72, 10.3.1994, p. 3.
