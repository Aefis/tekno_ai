shipping companies specifying the administering authority in respect of a shipping company in accordance with Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council (Text with EEA relevance) THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, Having regard to Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 October 2003 establishing a system for greenhouse gas emission allowance trading within the Union and amending Council Directive 96/61/EC (1), and in particular Article 3gf(2), point (a), thereof, Whereas: (1) Directive 2003/87/EC was amended recently by Directive (EU) 2023/959 of the European Parliament and of the Council (2) to include maritime transport emissions within the system for greenhouse gas emission allowance trading within the Union (the ‘EU ETS’). (2) Directive 2003/87/EC provides for one Member State to be responsible for each shipping company. Pursuant to Directive 2003/87/EC, the Member State responsible for administering a shipping company is referred to as the ‘administering authority in respect of a shipping company’. Article 3gf(1) and (3) of Directive 2003/87/EC and Articles 4 and 5 of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/2599 (3) govern the attribution of each shipping company to its administering authority in respect of a shipping company. The establishment of a list of shipping companies and their administering authority in respect of a shipping company (the ‘list’) aims at reducing the administrative burden on shipping companies and on Member States by providing information on which Member State will administer a particular shipping company. (3) The definition of ‘shipping company’ was introduced by Directive (EU) 2023/959 and included in the definition of ‘company’ in Regulation (EU) 2015/757 of the European Parliament and of the Council (4) by amending Regulation (EU) 2023/957 of the European Parliament and of the Council (5). Pursuant to Article 2 of Regulation (EU) 2023/957, the amended definition of ‘company’ is to apply from 1 January 2024. For the period from 2018 to 2023, port calls data per shipping companies are based on the definition of ‘company’ that was applicable until 31 December 2023 under Regulation (EU) 2015/757. (4) For the establishment of the list, the European Maritime Safety Agency developed an algorithm to be used to attribute each shipping company to a Member State, in accordance with the rules laid down in Article 3gf(1) and (3) of Directive 2003/87/EC and Articles 4 and 5 of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/2599. The algorithm has been applied on 20 November 2023, based on the information that was available on that date in Thetis MRV and in the Union maritime information and exchange system established by Directive 2002/59/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council (6) (‘SafeSeaNet’). The algorithm covered all the entities that had a company account with at least one ship registered under that account in Thetis MRV on 20 November 2023. Therefore, some of the entities in the list might not fall under the new definition of ‘shipping company’ laid down in Article 3, point (w), of Directive 2003/87/EC. (5) In order to facilitate the identification of shipping companies, the list includes, when available, the IMO unique company and registered owner identification number of each entity as recorded by entities in Thetis MRV. To ensure data consistency in terms of format, the few numbers that were recorded by entities deviating from the IMO identification scheme format are left blank. (6) Since the inclusion of a shipping company in the EU ETS is dependent upon the performance of a maritime transport activity listed in Annex I to Directive 2003/87/EC, it is not dependent on the shipping companies being included in the list, HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION: Article 1 The list of shipping companies referred to in Article 3gf(2), point (a), of Directive 2003/87/EC is established as set out in the Annex to this Decision. Article 2 This Decision shall enter into force on the third day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union. Done at Brussels, 30 January 2024.
