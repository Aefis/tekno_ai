(2) The Danish language version of Decision 2000/532/EC contains errors in the Annex, Section ‘Definitions’, points 2 and 4, Section ‘Assessment and classification’, point 1, fourth sentence, point 2, second paragraph, fifth indent, the introductory sentence and errors throughout the table in Section ‘List of Waste’. Thοse errors alter the meaning of the provisions. (3) The Greek language version of Decision 2000/532/EC contains errors in the Annex, Section ‘List of Waste’, the table, first column, rows ‘01 03 99’, ‘01 04 08’ and ‘06 02’ which alter the meaning of the provisions. (4) The Danish and Greek language versions of Decision 2000/532/EC should therefore be corrected accordingly. The other language versions are not affected, HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION: Article 1 (Does not concern the English language.) Article 2 This Decision shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union. Done at Brussels, 12 September 2023. For the Commission The President Ursula VON DER LEYEN (1) OJ L 312, 22.11.2008, p. 3. (2) Commission Decision 2014/955/EU of 18 December 2014 amending Decision 2000/532/EC on the list of waste pursuant to Directive 2008/98/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council (OJ L 370, 30.12.2014, p. 44). (3) Commission Decision 2000/532/EC of 3 May 2000 replacing Decision 94/3/EC establishing a list of wastes pursuant to Article 1(a) of Council Directive 75/442/EEC on waste and Council Decision 94/904/EC establishing a list of hazardous waste pursuant to Article 1(4) of Council Directive 91/689/EEC on hazardous waste (OJ L 226, 6.9.2000, p. 3).
