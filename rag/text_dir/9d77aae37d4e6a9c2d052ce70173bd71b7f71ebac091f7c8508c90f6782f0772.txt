(2) For reasons of clarity and legal certainty, the said Regulation should be expressly repealed, HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION: Article 1 Regulation (Euratom) No 2014/76 is hereby repealed. Article 2 This Regulation shall enter into force on the seventh day following its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union. This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States. Done at Brussels, 22 October 2004. For the Commission Loyola DE PALACIO Vice-President
