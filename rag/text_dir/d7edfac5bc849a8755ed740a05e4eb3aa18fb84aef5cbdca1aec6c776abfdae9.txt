In this context, the Council would welcome action by Member States to test the practical value of such approaches and to acquire experience of implementing them; INVITES the Commission to examine this question in greater detail and to submit, where necessary, in the light of the information supplied by the Member States and developments in relevant international practices, appropriate proposals with a view to the possible adjustment of the Community instrument; NOTES with great interest the intention of the Commission fully and actively to participate in the work of the Ad hoc Working Group of Experts of the United Nations Environment Programme dealing with the environmentally safe management of chemicals.
