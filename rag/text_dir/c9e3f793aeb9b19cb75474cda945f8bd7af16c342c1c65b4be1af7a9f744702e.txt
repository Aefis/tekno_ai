Whereas the Paris Convention on the prevention of marine pollution from land-based sources of 21 February 1974 provides for the drawing-up and implementation of programmes for eliminating or reducing this type of pollution in the north-east Atlantic; Whereas the Member States which take part in the intergovernmental meeting in Barcelona must ensure that the measures taken for the prevention of marine pollution from land-based sources are as consistent and rational as possible, RECOMMENDS THAT THESE MEMBER STATES (2): propose that on the agenda for the next session of the abovementioned intergovernmental meeting should be the preparation of a Protocol for the protection of the Mediterranean against marine pollution from land-based sources, the clauses of which shall be modelled, mutatis mutandis, on those of the Paris Convention of 21 February 1974. Done at Brussels, 19 December 1975. For the Commission Carlo SCARASCIA MUGNOZZA Vice-President (1)OJ No C 112, 20.12.1973. (2)The French Republic and the Italian Republic.
