enter corrections to the national allocation tables of Denmark, Germany, France, Croatia, Italy, Lithuania, Austria and Portugal into the Union Registry (C/2024/5877) THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, Having regard to Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 October 2003 establishing a system for greenhouse gas emission allowance trading within the Union and amending Council Directive 96/61/EC (1), Having regard to Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/1122 of 12 March 2019 supplementing Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the functioning of the Union Registry (2), and in particular Article 47(3), second subparagraph, thereof, Whereas: (1) Member States submitted to the Commission the list of installations in their territory pursuant to Article 11(1) of Directive 2003/87/EC. (2) In accordance with Article 2 of Commission Decision (EU) 2021/355 (3), the Commission raised no objections to the lists of installations covered by Directive 2003/87/EC as submitted by Belgium, Bulgaria, Czechia, Denmark, Germany, Estonia, Ireland, Greece, Spain, France, Croatia, Italy, Cyprus, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Hungary, the Netherlands, Austria, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovenia, Slovakia, Finland and Sweden, except as set out in Article 1 and Annexes of that Decision. (3) Member States determined and notified the preliminary annual amounts of free allowances per installation using the revised benchmark values determined in Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/447 (4), pursuant to Article 14(5) of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/331 (5). (4) In Article 1 of Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/927 (6), it was determined that no adjustment needed to be applied to the preliminary annual amounts of free allowances pursuant to Article 10a(5) of Directive 2003/87/EC, as demand did not exceed the maximum amount. (5) Denmark notified changes to its national allocation table, by letter dated 1 July 2024. The historical activity level of the district heating sub-installation was corrected for installation Dk-342. (6) Germany notified changes to its national allocation table, by letter dated 1 July 2024. The electricity exchangeability factors of the EAF carbon steel sub-installation and of the EAF high alloy steel sub-installation were corrected for installation DE-67. The historical activity levels of the heat sub-installations and district heating sub-installation were corrected for installation DE-201900. (7) France notified changes to its national allocation table, by letter dated 4 July 2024. Installation FR-217260 was reported in the initial national allocation table with the wrong identification number of the registry account. The new permanent number was replaced by identification number FR-461. The fuel sub-installation was deleted, and the historical activity level of the heat sub-installation was corrected for installation FR-90. The historical activity levels of the heat sub-installation and fuel sub-installation were corrected for installation FR-205489. The historical activity levels of the heat sub-installation and fuel sub-installation were corrected for installation FR-203838. (8) Croatia notified changes to its national allocation table, by letters dated 26 February 2024, and 27 June 2024. The electricity exchangeability factor of the EAF carbon steel sub-installation was corrected for installation HR-204295. The historical activity levels of the fuel sub-installation and process emissions sub-installation were corrected for installation HR-203853. The historical activity levels of the fuel sub-installation and process emissions sub-installation was corrected for installation HR-205104. The historical activity level of the dolime sub-installation was corrected for installation HR-205683. (9) Italy notified changes to its national allocation table, by letter dated 28 February 2024. Installation IT-219761 was reintroduced to free allocation under the Emissions Trading System with corrected registry identification number. The new permanent number was replaced by identification number IT-new-2168. (10) Lithuania notified changes to its national allocation table, by letter dated 20 February 2024. The historical activity level of the district heating sub-installation was corrected for installation LT-38. (11) Austria notified changes to its national allocation table, by letters dated 8 April 2024, 23 May 2024, and 11 June 2024. The historical activity levels of the heat sub-installations were corrected for installation AT-47. The historical activity level of the heat sub-installation was corrected for installation AT-142. The historical activity level of the heat sub-installation was corrected for installation AT-27. The historical activity levels of the heat sub-installation and fuel sub-installation were corrected for installation AT-28. (12) Portugal notified changes to its national allocation table, by letter dated 14 June 2024. The historical activity level of the heat sub-installation was corrected for installation PT-59. (13) The notified national allocation tables are in conformity with Article 10c of Directive 2003/87/EC, Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/331 and Decision (EU) 2021/355. (14) It is therefore appropriate to instruct the central administrator to enter those corrections into the Union Registry, HAS DECIDED AS FOLLOWS: Sole Article The central administrator of the Union Registry is hereby instructed to enter corrections to the national allocation tables of Denmark, Germany, France, Croatia, Italy, Lithuania, Austria and Portugal, with the final annual amounts for the transitional free allocation of emission allowances for the period from 2021 to 2025, as set out in the Annex, into the Union Registry.
