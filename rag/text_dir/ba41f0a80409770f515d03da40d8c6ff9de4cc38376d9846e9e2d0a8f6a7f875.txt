(2) Decision 2003/43/EC needs to be further adapted to take account of technical progress regarding wood-based panels. (3) Decision 2003/43/EC should therefore be amended accordingly. (4) The measures provided for in this Decision are in accordance with the opinion of the Standing Committee on Construction, HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION: Article 1 The Annex to Decision 2003/43/EC is amended as set out in the Annex to this Decision. Article 2 This Decision is addressed to the Member States. Done at Brussels, 15 May 2007. For the Commission Günter VERHEUGEN Vice-President (1) OJ L 40, 11.2.1989, p. 12.
