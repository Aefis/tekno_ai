(2) Aleurocanthus spiniferus (Quaintance) (‘the specified pest’) is included in that list, as it is known to occur in certain areas in Greece, France, Croatia and Italy affecting a broad range of plant species, such as plants of citrus, apples, vine, pomegranates, ornamentals. (3) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/1927 (3) establishes measures for the containment of the specified pest within certain demarcated areas for containment and listed these areas in Greece, France, Croatia and Italy. (4) Since the adoption of that Regulation, surveys carried out pursuant to Article 19(1) of Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 have shown that the eradication of the specified pest in the demarcated areas for eradication in the counties of Dubrovnik Neretva, Primorje-Gorski and Split Dalmatia in Croatia, in Occitanie and Provence Alpes Côte d’Azur in France, in the Regional Units of Achaia, Argolida and Corinthia, and the Region of Attica in Greece, and in the regions Abruzzo, Basilicata, Emilia-Romagna, Lazio, Lombardy, Marche, Apulia, Sicily and Tuscany in Italy, is no longer possible. (5) In addition, as the buffer zone located in Provence Alpes Côte d’Azur extends into the territory of Italy, covering part of the municipality of Ventimiglia, that part of the buffer zone should also be included in Annex I to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/1927. (6) Consequently, Annex I to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/1927 should be amended to include those new demarcated areas for containment. (7) The measures provided for in this Regulation are in accordance with the opinion of the Standing Committee on Plants, Animals, Food and Feed, HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION: Article 1 Amendment of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/1927 Annex I to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/1927 is replaced by the text in the Annex to this Regulation. Article 2 Entry into force This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union. This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States. Done at Brussels, 2 June 2025.
