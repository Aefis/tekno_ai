DECISION of 15 April 2021 instructing the central administrator to enter changes to the national allocation tables of Denmark, Germany, Spain, France, Italy, Latvia, Hungary, the Netherlands, Portugal, Romania, Finland and Sweden into the European Union Transaction Log (2021/C 272 I/01) THE COMMISSION OF THE EUROPEAN UNION, Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, Having regard to Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 October 2003 establishing a system for greenhouse gas emission allowance trading within the Union and amending Council Directive 96/61/EC (1), Having regard to Commission Regulation (EU) No 389/2013 of 2 May 2013 establishing a Union Registry pursuant to Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council, Decisions No 280/2004/EC and No 406/2009/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Commission Regulations (EU) No 920/2010 and (EU) No 1193/2011 (2), and in particular Article 52(2), second subparagraph thereof, Whereas: (1) On 27 April 2011, the Commission adopted Decision 2011/278/EU (3) determining transitional Union-wide rules for harmonised free allocation of emission allowances pursuant to Directive 2003/87/EC, Article 10a. Pursuant to Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/331 (4), Article 27, Decision 2011/278/EU continues to apply to allocations relating to the period prior to 1 January 2021. All notified allocations referred to in the present decision relate to the free allocation of emission allowances for the period from 2013 to 2020. (2) On 5 September 2013, the Commission adopted Decision 2013/448/EU (5) concerning national implementation measures for the transitional free allocation of greenhouse gas emission allowances for the period 2013-20. (3) By Decision 2014/9/EU (6), the Commission amended Decisions 2010/2/EU (7) and 2011/278/EU as regards the list of sectors and subsectors which are deemed to be exposed to a significant risk of carbon leakage (hereinafter ‘carbon leakage list’). (4) By Decisions C(2013) 9281 (8), C(2014) 123 (9), C(2014) 674 (10) and C(2014) 1167 (11), the Commission instructed the central administrator to enter the national allocation tables and the revised national allocation tables of Belgium, Bulgaria, Czechia, Denmark, Germany, Estonia, Ireland, Greece, Spain, France, Croatia, Italy, Cyprus, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Hungary, the Netherlands, Austria, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovenia, Slovakia, Finland and Sweden into the European Union Transaction Log. (5) Pursuant to Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, Article 52(2), first subparagraph, Denmark notified to the Commission, by letter dated 11 February 2021, changes to its national allocation table to provide free allocation of emission allowances to new entrants. (6) Pursuant to Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, Article 52(2), first subparagraph, Germany notified to the Commission, by letter dated 30 March 2021, changes to its national allocation table to provide free allocation of emission allowances to new entrants. (7) Pursuant to Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, Article 52(2), first subparagraph, Spain notified to the Commission, by letter dated 19 March 2021, changes to its national allocation table to provide free allocation of emission allowances to new entrants and changes due to significant capacity reductions, partial cessations, subsequent recovery of activity levels as well as amendments to the carbon leakage list. (8) Pursuant to Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, Article 52(2), first subparagraph, France notified to the Commission, by letter dated 31 March 2021, changes to its national allocation table to provide free allocation of emission allowances to new entrants and changes due to significant capacity reductions, partial cessations, subsequent recovery of activity levels as well as amendments to the carbon leakage list. (9) Pursuant to Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, Article 52(2), first subparagraph, Italy notified to the Commission, by letter dated 31 March 2021, changes to its national allocation table to provide free allocation of emission allowances to new entrants and changes due to significant capacity reductions, partial cessations, subsequent recovery of activity levels as well as amendments to the carbon leakage list. (10) Pursuant to Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, Article 52(2), first subparagraph, Latvia notified to the Commission, by letter dated 30 March 2021, changes due to significant capacity reductions, partial cessations, subsequent recovery of activity levels as well as amendments to the carbon leakage list. (11) Pursuant to Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, Article 52(2), first subparagraph, Hungary notified to the Commission, by letters dated 11 March 2021 and 31 March 2021, changes to its national allocation table to provide free allocation of emission allowances to new entrants and changes due to significant capacity reductions, partial cessations, subsequent recovery of activity levels as well as amendments to the carbon leakage list. (12) Pursuant to Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, Article 52(2), first subparagraph, the Netherlands notified to the Commission, by letter dated 08 March 2021, changes due to significant capacity reductions, partial cessations, subsequent recovery of activity levels as well as amendments to the carbon leakage list. (13) Pursuant to Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, Article 52(2), first subparagraph, Portugal notified to the Commission, by letter dated 25 January 2021, changes to its national allocation table to provide free allocation of emission allowances to new entrants. (14) Pursuant to Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, Article 52(2), first subparagraph, Romania notified to the Commission, by letter dated 19 March 2021, changes due to significant capacity reductions, partial cessations, subsequent recovery of activity levels as well as amendments to the carbon leakage list. (15) Pursuant to Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, Article 52(2), first subparagraph, Finland notified to the Commission, by letter dated 06 April 2021, changes to its national allocation table to provide free allocation of emission allowances to new entrants and changes due to significant capacity reductions, partial cessations, subsequent recovery of activity levels as well as amendments to the carbon leakage list. (16) Pursuant to Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, Article 52(2), first subparagraph, Sweden notified to the Commission, by letter dated 08 February 2021, changes due to significant capacity reductions, partial cessations, subsequent recovery of activity levels as well as amendments to the carbon leakage list. (17) The Commission considers that the notified changes to the national allocation tables of Denmark, Germany, Spain, France, Italy, Latvia, Hungary, the Netherlands, Portugal, Romania, Finland and Sweden are in conformity with Decision 2011/278/EU. The central administrator should therefore be instructed to enter those changes into the European Union Transaction Log, HAS DECIDED AS FOLLOWS: Sole Article The central administrator shall enter the changes to the national allocation tables of Denmark, Germany, Spain, France, Italy, Latvia, Hungary, the Netherlands, Portugal, Romania, Finland and Sweden set out in Annexes I to XII respectively into the European Union Transaction Log.
