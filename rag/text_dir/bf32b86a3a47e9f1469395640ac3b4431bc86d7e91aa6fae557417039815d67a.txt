European Union, Having regard to Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 October 2003 establishing a system for greenhouse gas emission allowance trading within the Union and amending Council Directive 96/61/EC (1), Having regard to Commission Regulation (EU) No 389/2013 of 2 May 2013 establishing a Union Registry pursuant to Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council, Decisions No 280/2004/EC and No 406/2009/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Commission Regulations (EU) No 920/2010 and (EU) No 1193/2011 (2), and in particular the second subparagraph of Article 52(2) thereof, Whereas: (1) On 27 April 2011, the Commission adopted Decision 2011/278/EU (3) determining transitional Union-wide rules for harmonised free allocation of emission allowances pursuant to Article 10a of Directive 2003/87/EC. (2) On 5 September 2013, the Commission adopted Decision 2013/448/EU (4) concerning national implementation measures for the transitional free allocation of greenhouse gas emission allowances for the period 2013-2020. (3) By Decision 2014/9/EU (5), the Commission amended Decisions 2010/2/EU (6) and 2011/278/EU as regards the list of sectors and subsectors which are deemed to be exposed to a significant risk of carbon leakage (hereinafter ‘carbon leakage list’). (4) By Decisions C(2013) 9281 (7), C(2014) 123 (8), C(2014) 674 (9) and C(2014) 1167 (10), the Commission instructed the Central Administrator of the European Union Transaction Log to enter the national allocation tables and the revised national allocation tables of Belgium, Bulgaria, Czechia, Denmark, Germany, Estonia, Ireland, Greece, Spain, France, Croatia, Italy, Cyprus, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Hungary, the Netherlands, Austria, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovenia, Slovakia, Finland, Sweden and the United Kingdom into the European Union Transaction Log. (5) Pursuant to the first subparagraph of Article 52(2) of Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, Bulgaria notified to the Commission, by letters dated 23 June 2020 and 24 June 2020, changes to its national allocation table to provide free allocation of emission allowances to new entrants. (6) Pursuant to the first subparagraph of Article 52(2) of Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, Czechia notified to the Commission, by letter dated 24 June 2020, changes to its national allocation table due to significant capacity reductions, partial cessations, subsequent recovery of activity levels as well as amendments to the carbon leakage list. (7) Pursuant to the first subparagraph of Article 52(2) of Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, Denmark notified to the Commission, by letter dated 07 July 2020, changes to its national allocation table to provide free allocation of emission allowances to new entrants. (8) Pursuant to the first subparagraph of Article 52(2) of Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, Germany notified to the Commission, by letters dated 16 June 2020, 22 June 2020 and 23 June 2020, changes to its national allocation table to provide free allocation of emission allowances to new entrants and changes due to significant capacity reductions, partial cessations, subsequent recovery of activity levels as well as amendments to the carbon leakage list. (9) Pursuant to the first subparagraph of Article 52(2) of Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, Spain notified to the Commission, by letter dated 26 June 2020, changes to its national allocation table due to significant capacity reductions, partial cessations, subsequent recovery of activity levels as well as amendments to the carbon leakage list. (10) Pursuant to the first subparagraph of Article 52(2) of Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, France notified to the Commission, by letters dated 06 July 2020 and 07 July 2020, changes to its national allocation table to provide free allocation of emission allowances to new entrants and changes due to significant capacity reductions, partial cessations, subsequent recovery of activity levels as well as amendments to the carbon leakage list. (11) Pursuant to the first subparagraph of Article 52(2) of Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, Italy notified to the Commission, by letter dated 07 July 2020, changes to its national allocation table to provide free allocation of emission allowances to new entrants and changes due to significant capacity reductions, partial cessations, subsequent recovery of activity levels as well as amendments to the carbon leakage list. (12) Pursuant to the first subparagraph of Article 52(2) of Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, Latvia notified to the Commission, by letter dated 02 April 2020, changes to its national allocation table due to significant capacity reductions, partial cessations, subsequent recovery of activity levels as well as amendments to the carbon leakage list. (13) Pursuant to the first subparagraph of Article 52(2) of Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, the Netherlands notified to the Commission, by letters dated 29 June 2020, 30 June 2020 and 07 July 2020, changes to its national allocation table to provide free allocation of emission allowances to new entrants and changes due to significant capacity reductions, partial cessations, subsequent recovery of activity levels as well as amendments to the carbon leakage list. (14) Pursuant to the first subparagraph of Article 52(2) of Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, Austria notified to the Commission, by letters dated 07 May 2020 and 25 June 2020, changes to its national allocation due to significant capacity reductions, partial cessations, subsequent recovery of activity levels as well as amendments to the carbon leakage list. (15) Pursuant to the first subparagraph of Article 52(2) of Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, Romania notified to the Commission, by letter dated 30 June 2020, changes to its national allocation table due to significant capacity reductions, partial cessations, subsequent recovery of activity levels as well as amendments to the carbon leakage list. (16) Pursuant to the first subparagraph of Article 52(2) of Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, Sweden notified to the Commission, by letter dated 06 July 2020, changes to its national allocation table to provide free allocation of emission allowances to new entrants and changes due to significant capacity reductions, partial cessations, subsequent recovery of activity levels as well as amendments to the carbon leakage list. (17) Pursuant to the first subparagraph of Article 52(2) of Regulation (EU) No 389/2013, the United Kingdom notified to the Commission, by letters dated 03 June 2020, 04 June 2020, 09 June 2020, 12 June 2020, 25 June 2020, 29 June 2020, 30 June 2020 and 01 July 2020, changes to its national allocation table to provide free allocation of emission allowances to new entrants and changes due to significant capacity reductions, partial cessations, subsequent recovery of activity levels as well as amendments to the carbon leakage list.
