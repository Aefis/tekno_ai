Company, with registered offices at 11711 Memorial Drive, Suite 258, Houston, Texas 77024 (United States), asked for an exclusive licence to prospect for liquid and gaseous hydrocarbons, entitled "Permis de Lavignolle", covering an area of roughly 215 sq km, partly in the département of Gironde, for a period of four years. The perimeter of the licence requested is made up of the meridian and parallel arcs joining the vertices defined below by their geographical coordinates, the original meridian being that of Paris. >TABLE> Interested companies may submit a competing application within a period of 90 days from publication of this notice in accordance with the procedure summarised in "Notice regarding the granting of mining rights for hydrocarbons in France", as published in the Official Journal of the European Communities C 374 of 30 December 1994, p. 11, and established by Decree 95-427 of 19 April 1995 regarding mining rights (Journal officiel de la République française of 22 April 1995).
