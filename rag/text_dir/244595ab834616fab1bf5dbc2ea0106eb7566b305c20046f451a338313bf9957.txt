The Annex to Commission Regulation (EC) No 1418/2007 (2) should therefore be amended to take this into account, HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION: Article 1 The Annex to Regulation (EC) No 1418/2007 is amended in accordance with the Annex to this Regulation. Article 2 This Regulation shall enter into force on the 14th day following its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union. This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States. Done at Brussels, 8 July 2011.
