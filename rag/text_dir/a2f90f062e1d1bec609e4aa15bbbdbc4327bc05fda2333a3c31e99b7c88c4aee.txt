(2) On 14 February 2024, the Political and Security Committee agreed that the Operation Plan (OPLAN), including the Rules of Engagement, for EUNAVFOR ASPIDES should be approved. (3) Following the recommendation of the EU Operation Commander, EUNAVFOR ASPIDES should be launched on 19 February 2024, HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION: Article 1 The OPLAN, including the Rules of Engagement, for EUNAVFOR ASPIDES is hereby approved. Article 2 EUNAVFOR ASPIDES shall be launched on 19 February 2024. Article 3 This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption. Done at Brussels, 19 February 2024. For the Council The President J.
