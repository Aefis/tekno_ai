Technical performance: for: ‘Hydraulic fluids shall at least meet the technical performance criteria laid down in ISO 15380, Tables 2 to 5. Greases shall be ‘fit for purpose’. Chain-saw oils shall at least meet the technical performance criteria laid down in the RAL-UZ 48 of the Blue Angel. Concrete release agents and other total loss lubricants shall be fit for purpose. Two-stroke oils shall at least meet the technical performance criteria laid down in ‘NMMA Certification for two-stroke cycle gasoline engine lubricants’ of NMMA TC-W3. Assessment and verification of criterion 6 The applicant shall provide the competent body with a declaration of compliance with this criterion, together with related documentation.’; read: ‘Hydraulic fluids shall at least meet the technical performance criteria laid down in ISO 15380, Tables 2 to 5. Greases shall be ‘fit for purpose’. Chain-saw oils shall at least meet the technical performance criteria laid down in the RAL-UZ 48 of the Blue Angel. Concrete release agents and other total loss lubricants shall be fit for purpose. Two-stroke oils for marine applications shall at least meet the technical performance criteria laid down in ‘NMMA Certification for Two-Stroke Cycle Gasoline Engine Lubricants’ of NMMA TC-W3. Two-stroke oils for terrestrial applications shall at least meet the EGD level of technical performance criteria laid down in ISO 13738:2000. Assessment and verification of criterion 6 The applicant shall provide the competent body with a declaration of compliance with this criterion, together with related documentation.’.
