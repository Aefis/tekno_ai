By letter of 2 September 1980, pursuant to Article 3 of the abovementioned Directive, the Permanent Representation of the Federal Republic of Germany forwarded to the Commission the draft of a third "Verordnung zur Änderung der allgemeinen Lotsordnung". 2. Following examination of this document, the Commission has ascertained that the measures proposed by the German Government fully implement the provisions contained in the Directive in question. Done at Brussels, 14 July 1981. For the Commission Georges CONTOGEORGIS Member of the Commission (1) OJ No L 315, 11.12.1979, p. 16.
