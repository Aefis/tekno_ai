(3) The international credit entitlements determined in accordance with Regulation (EU) No 1123/2013 are updated automatically every year by the central administrator of the Union Registry on the basis of verified emissions. In the case of new stationary installations and new aircraft operators, there is no initial value of the international credit entitlements.
