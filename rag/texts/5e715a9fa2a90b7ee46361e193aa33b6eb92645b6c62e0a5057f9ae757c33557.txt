COMMISSION RECOMMENDATION of 17 August 2006 on the prevention and reduction of Fusarium toxins in cereals and cereal products (Text with EEA relevance) (2006/583/EC) THE COMMISSION OF THE EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES, Having regard to the Treaty establishing the European Community, and in particular the second indent of Article 211 thereof, Whereas: HEREBY RECOMMENDS: That the Member States should take into account the uniform principles set out in the Annex when adopting measures directed at operators in the cereal chain in order to control and manage contamination of cereals by Fusarium toxins.
