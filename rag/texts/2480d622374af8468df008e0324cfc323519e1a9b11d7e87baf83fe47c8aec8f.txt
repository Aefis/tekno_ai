Avis juridique important Corrigendum to Commission Decision 2001/171/EC of 19 February 2001 establishing the conditions for a derogation for glass packaging in relation to the heavy metal concentration levels established in Directive 94/62/EC on packaging and packaging waste (OJ L 62 of 2.3.2001) Official Journal L 272 , 10/10/2002 P. 0035 - 0035 Corrigendum to Commission Decision 2001/171/EC of 19 February 2001 establishing the conditions for a derogation for glass packaging in relation to the heavy metal concentration levels established in Directive 94/62/EC on packaging and packaging waste (Official Journal of the European Communities L 62 of 2 March 2001) On page 20, Article 5: for: "Where the average heavy metals concentration levels ..., the manufacturer or this authorised representative ...", read: "Where the average heavy metals concentration levels ..., the manufacturer or his authorised representative ...".
