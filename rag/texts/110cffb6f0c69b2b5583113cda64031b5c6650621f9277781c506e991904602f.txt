Avis juridique important Communication from the French Government concerning Directive 94/22/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 30 May 1994 on the conditions for granting and using authorisations for the prospection, exploration and production of hydrocarbons (Text with EEA relevance) Official Journal C 044 , 10/02/2001 P. 0012 - 0012 Communication from the French Government concerning Directive 94/22/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 30 May 1994 on the conditions for granting and using authorisations for the prospection, exploration and production of hydrocarbons(1) (2001/C 44/05) (Text with EEA relevance) Notice regarding an application for an exclusive licence to prospect for liquid and gaseous hydrocarbons, designated as "Permis de Caravelle" By request of 10 July 2000, the company RSM Production Corporation, with registered offices at 5000 S. Quebec Street, Suite 500, Denver, Colorado 80237 (USA), asked for an exclusive five-year licence to prospect for liquid and gaseous hydrocarbons, designated as "Permis de Caravelle", for a currently undefined area of seabed off the overseas department of Martinique. The licence covers an area bounded by a line sequentially joining the points defined below by their geographical coordinates, the prime meridian being that of Greenwich: >TABLE> The dividing line between France and Dominica is the line joining points 1 and 2. The lines of longitude and latitude join points 2 to 11 sequentially as defined above by their geographical coordinates, the prime meridian being that of Greenwich. The dividing line between France and Barbados, to be defined, is the line joining points 11 and 12. The dividing line between France and St Lucia is the line joining points 12 and 13. The boundary of the western exclusive economic zone is the line joining point 13 to point 1. Interested companies may submit a competing application within a period of 90 days from publication of this notice in accordance with the procedure summarised in "Notice regarding the granting of mining rights for hydrocarbons in France", as published in Official Journal of the European Communities C 374 of 30 December 1994, p. 11, and established by Decree 95-427 of 19 April 1995 regarding mining rights (Journal officiel de la République française of 22 April 1995).
