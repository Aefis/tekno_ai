COMMISSION DECISION of 19 April 2023 on instructing the Central Administrator of the European Union Transaction Log to enter changes to the national aviation allocation table of Italy for 2022 and 2023 into the European Union Transaction Log (2023/C 226/06) THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, Having regard to Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 October 2003 establishing a system for greenhouse gas emission allowance trading within the Union and amending Council Directive 96/61/EC ( 1 ) , Having regard to Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/1122 of 12 March 2019 supplementing Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the functioning of the Union Registry ( 2 ) and in particular Article 49(2) thereof, Whereas: HAS DECIDED AS FOLLOWS: Sole Article The Central Administrator of the European Union Transaction Log shall enter the changes to the national allocation table of Italy set out in Annex I. Done at Brussels, 19 April 2023. For the Commission Frans TIMMERMANS Executive Vice-President ( 1 ) OJ L 275, 25.10.2003, p. 32 . ( 2 ) OJ L 177, 2.7.2019, p. 3 .
