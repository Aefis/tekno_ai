Article 3 This Decision is addressed to the Member States. Done at Brussels, 29 April 2004. The following forms distinguish between items that are legally required to be reported and items that are voluntarily reported by the Member State. Voluntary items are printed in italic. Many of the forms below contain an indefinite number of rows or columns to be filled in. In the form description, the number of empty rows or columns to be filled in is then limited to three and a dashed borderline indicates that the form should be extended as needed. In addition to the forms, which are to be filled in by the Member State, some tables are also provided.
