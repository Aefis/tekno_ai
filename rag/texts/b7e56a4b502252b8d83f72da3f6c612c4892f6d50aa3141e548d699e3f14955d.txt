COMMISSION OPINION OF 10 JUNE 1981 ADDRESSED TO THE IRISH GOVERNMENT ON THE IMPLEMENTATION OF : - COUNCIL DIRECTIVE 79/116/EEC CONCERNING MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS FOR CERTAIN TANKERS ENTERING OR LEAVING COMMUNITY PORTS ( 1 ) - COUNCIL DIRECTIVE 79/1034/EEC AMENDING THE FOREGOING DIRECTIVE ( 2 ) ( ONLY THE ENGLISH TEXT IS AUTHENTIC ) ( 81/454/EEC ) 1 . IN JULY 1980 , IN ACCORDANCE WITH ARTICLE 3 OF EACH OF THE ABOVEMENTIONED DIRECTIVES , THE IRISH PERMANENT REPRESENTATION TO THE EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES SENT THE COMMISSION A COPY OF A DRAFT REGULATION GIVING EFFECT TO THE PROVISIONS OF THESE TWO DIRECTIVES . 2 . ON EXAMINING THE DOCUMENT , THE COMMISSION FOUND THAT IT CONTAINED NO PROVISIONS IMPLEMENTING THE FOLLOWING PROVISIONS OF DIRECTIVE 79/116/EEC : - ARTICLE 1 ( 1 ) B ( III ), WHICH REFERS TO THE USE OF THE SERVICES PROVIDED BY RADAR STATIONS IN CONDITIONS OF RESTRICTED VISIBILITY , - PART OF SECTION D OF THE TANKER CHECK LIST . HOWEVER , DEROGATIONS ON THESE POINTS ARE PERMITTED BY THE PROVISIONS OF THE DIRECTIVE ITSELF . 3 . NOR DOES THE DRAFT REGULATION INCORPORATE ARTICLE 1 ( 2 ) WHICH STATES THAT IF THE PILOT LEARNS OF ANY DEFICIENCIES WHICH MAY PREJUDICE THE SAFE NAVIGATION OF THE VESSEL HE SHALL IMMEDIATELY REPORT THEM TO THE COMPETENT AUTHORITY . HOWEVER , THE COMMISSION HAS BEEN INFORMED BY THE IRISH GOVERNMENT THAT THE PILOT IS ALREADY REQUIRED TO DO THIS UNDER IRISH LAW . 4 . AS THE OTHER PROVISIONS CONFORM WITH THE OBLIGATIONS LAID DOWN BY THE DIRECTIVE , THE COMMISSION HEREBY DELIVERS A FAVOURABLE OPINION ON THE DRAFT REGULATION SUBMITTED TO IT . DONE AT BRUSSELS , 10 JUNE 1981 . FOR THE COMMISSION GEORGES CONTOGEORGIS MEMBER OF THE COMMISSION
