ANNEX II Providing practical guidance on the application of the voluntary sustainability reporting standard for small and medium-sized undertakings contained in Annex I of this Recommendation This Annex provides practical guidance on the application of the voluntary sustainability reporting standard for small and medium-sized undertakings (VSME) laid down in Annex I of this Recommendation. This Annex provides guidance which clarifies the application of certain sustainability disclosure requirements contained in the voluntary sustainability reporting standard for small and medium-sized undertakings. Undertakings and other stakeholders may also wish to consult the implementation guidance on ESRS published by EFRAG. Through the provision of the guidance contained in this Annex, the Commission intends to facilitate the application of the disclosure requirements by small and medium-sized undertakings in a cost-effective way and to ensure the usability and comparability of the reported sustainability information. By providing greater clarity and certainty to companies, this guidance will contribute to the Commission's objective of simplifying sustainability reporting requirements and reducing administrative burdens on undertakings associated with sustainability reporting. The guidance contained in this Annex does not extend or add to in any way the provisions laid down in the Commission Recommendation nor in the voluntary sustainability reporting standard for small and medium-sized undertakings in Annex I of that Recommendation. This Annex is merely intended to assist undertakings in the practical implementation of the relevant disclosure requirements. Basic Module: Guidance 1.1.
