Corrigendum to Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/2055 of 25 September 2023 amending Annex XVII to Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning the Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals (REACH) as regards synthetic polymer microparticles ( Official Journal of the European Union L 238 of 27 September 2023 ) On page 77, in paragraph 1 of column 2 of the table in the Annex, amending Annex XVII to Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006: ‘Shall not be placed on the market as substances on their own or, where the synthetic polymer microparticles are present to confer a sought-after characteristic, in mixtures in a concentration equal to or greater than 0,01 % by weight.’, ‘Shall not be placed on the market on their own or, where the synthetic polymer microparticles are present to confer a sought-after characteristic, in mixtures in a concentration equal to or greater than 0,01 % by weight.’. On page 78, in paragraph 4(a) of column 2 of the table in the Annex, amending Annex XVII to Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006: ‘synthetic polymer microparticles, as substances on their own or in mixtures, for use at industrial sites.’, ‘synthetic polymer microparticles on their own or in mixtures, for use at industrial sites.’. On page 78, in the first sub-paragraph of paragraph 5 of column 2 of the table in the Annex, amending Annex XVII to Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006: ‘Paragraph 1 shall not apply to the placing on the market of the following synthetic polymer microparticles, as substances on their own or in mixtures:’, ‘Paragraph 1 shall not apply to the placing on the market of the following synthetic polymer microparticles on their own or in mixtures:’. On page 79, in paragraph 7(b) of column 2 of the table in the Annex, amending Annex XVII to Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006: ‘The synthetic polymer microparticles supplied is subject to conditions laid down by entry 78 of Annex XVII to Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council.’, ‘The synthetic polymer microparticles supplied are subject to conditions laid down by entry 78 of Annex XVII to Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council.’.
