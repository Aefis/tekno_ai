Avis juridique important 81/815/EEC: Commission opinion of 28 September 1981 addressed to the Belgian Government with regard to the implementation of: - Council Directive 79/116/EEC concerning minimum requirements for certain tankers entering or leaving Community ports, - Council Directive 79/1034/EEC amending the above Directive Official Journal L 301 , 22/10/1981 P. 0048 - 0048 COMMISSION OPINION of 28 September 1981 addressed to the Belgian Government with regard to the implementation of: - Council Directive 79/116/EEC (1) concerning minimum requirements for certain tankers entering or leaving Community ports; - Council Directive 79/1034/EEC (2) amending the above Directive (81/815/EEC) 1. In a letter dated 19 December 1980, the Belgian Permanent Representation to the European Communities sent to the Commission, pursuant to Article 3 of both the above Directives, a draft Royal Decree designed to implement the provisions of the Directives. 2. Having examined the draft instrument, the Commission finds that Article 1 (1) (B) (iv) of Directive 79/116/EEC regarding the use of pilots in accordance with practice and with the regulations determined by the competent authorities is not implemented by the draft Royal Decree. The Commission has been informed by the Belgian Government in this connection that this requirement is met by another national provision. 3. In addition, the draft Royal Decree does not implement: - Article 1 (2) of the Directive regarding the report by the pilot to the competent authority on any deficiencies which may prejudice the safe navigation of the vessel, or - Article 2 of the Directive regarding the taking of appropriate measures to inform another Member State as soon as possible of the risk of a hazard being posed to certain maritime and coastal zones. The Belgian Government has assured the Commission that, once the Royal Decree has been adopted, appropriate arrangements will be made to ensure that the competent authorities rigorously apply the provisions referred to above. These arrangements will be communicated to those authorities in a departmental circular, a copy of which will be sent to the Commission for information. 4. Subject to the foregoing, the Commission hereby delivers a favourable opinion on the draft Royal Decree submitted to it. Done at Brussels, 28 September 1981.
