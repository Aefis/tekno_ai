COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) 2024/2405 of 12 September 2024 cancelling the Union authorisation for the single biocidal product ‘SchwabEX-Guard’ in accordance with Regulation (EU) No 528/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/114 (Text with EEA relevance) THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 528/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 May 2012 concerning the making available on the market and use of biocidal products ( 1 ) , and in particular Article 49 thereof, Whereas: HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION: Article 1 The Union authorisation for the single biocidal product ‘SchwabEX-Guard’ granted by Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/114 to Sumitomo Chemical Agro Europe SAS with the authorisation number EU-0025436-0000 is cancelled. Article 2 Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/114 is repealed. Article 3 The biocidal product ‘SchwabEX-Guard’ shall no longer be made available on the market from 3 April 2025 and existing stocks of such biocidal product shall no longer be used from 3 October 2025. Article 4 This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union . This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States. Done at Brussels, 12 September 2024.
