COUNCIL DECISION (EU) 2022/2572 of 19 December 2022 requesting the Commission to submit a study complementing the impact assessment of the proposal for a Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council on the sustainable use of plant protection products and amending Regulation (EU) 2021/2115 of the European Parliament and of the Council, and to propose follow-up actions, if appropriate in view of the outcomes of the study THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, and in particular Article 241 thereof, Whereas: HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION: Article 1 1. The Council requests that the Commission submit, as soon as possible to facilitate the ongoing legislative process, but at the latest by 28 June 2023 a study complementing the existing impact assessment of the proposal for a Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council on the sustainable use of plant protection products and amending Regulation (EU) 2021/2115 on the following aspects: 2. The Council requests that the Commission propose any follow-up action justified in light of the outcome of the study. Article 2 This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union . Done at Brussels, 19 December 2022. For the Council The President J.
