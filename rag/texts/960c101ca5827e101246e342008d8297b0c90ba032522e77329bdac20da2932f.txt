COMMISSION REGULATION (EURATOM) No 1841/2004 of 22 October 2004 repealing Regulation (Euratom) No 2014/76 on the support of projects concerning uranium-prospecting programmes within the territories of the Member States THE COMMISSION OF THE EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES, Having regard to the Treaty establishing the European Atomic Energy Community, and in particular Article 70 thereof, Whereas: HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION: Article 1 Regulation (Euratom) No 2014/76 is hereby repealed. Article 2 This Regulation shall enter into force on the seventh day following its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union . This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States. Done at Brussels, 22 October 2004. For the Commission Loyola DE PALACIO Vice-President ( 1 ) OJ L 221, 14.8.1976, p. 17 .
