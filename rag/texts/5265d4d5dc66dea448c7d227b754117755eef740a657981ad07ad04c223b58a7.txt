COUNCIL DECISION (EU) 2025/1315 of 17 June 2025 on the position to be taken on behalf of the European Union concerning the nomination of a Union representative to the Board of the Fund for Responding to Loss and Damage THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION, Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, and in particular Article 209, in conjunction with Article 218(9) thereof, Having regard to the proposal from the European Commission, Whereas: HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION: Article 1 The position to be taken on the Union’s behalf concerning the nomination of a Union representative to the Board of the Fund for Responding to Loss and Damage and the sharing of seats between the representatives of the Commission and the Member States is attached to this Decision. Article 2 This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption. Done at Luxembourg, 17 June 2025. For the Council The President P. HENNIG-KLOSKA ( 1 ) OJ L 282, 19.10.2016, p. 4 . Position to be taken concerning the nomination of a Union representative to the Board of the Fund for Responding to Loss and Damage The Union, represented by the Commission, shall seek a seat on the Board of the Fund for Responding to Loss and Damage (the ‘Board’) established by the parties to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change and to the Paris Agreement.
