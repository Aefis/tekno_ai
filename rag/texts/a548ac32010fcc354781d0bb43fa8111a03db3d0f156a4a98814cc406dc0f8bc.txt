COUNCIL DECISION (EU) 2025/1293 of 19 June 2025 authorising the Commission to participate, on behalf of the European Union, in the negotiations on an amendment to Article 1, point (a), of the Convention for the Protection of the Marine Environment of the North-East Atlantic THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION, Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, and in particular Article 192(1), in conjunction with Article 218(3) and (4) thereof, Having regard to the recommendation from the European Commission, Whereas: HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION: Article 1 The Commission is hereby authorised to participate, on behalf of the Union, in the negotiations within the Commission of the Convention for the Protection of the Marine Environment of the North-East Atlantic (the ‘Convention’) on an amendment to Article 1, point (a), of the Convention aimed at including the waters surrounding Macaronesia (Madeira and the Canary Islands) in the maritime area governed by the Convention. Article 2 The negotiations shall be conducted on the basis of the negotiating directives set out in the addendum to this Decision. Article 3 The negotiations shall be conducted in consultation with the Council Working Party on the Environment, which is hereby designated as the special committee within the meaning of Article 218(4) of the Treaty. Article 4 This Decision is addressed to the Commission. Done at Luxembourg, 19 June 2025. For the Council The President A. DZIEMIANOWICZ-BĄK ( 1 ) Council Decision 98/249/EC of 7 October 1997 on the conclusion of the Convention for the protection of the marine environment of the north-east Atlantic ( OJ L 104, 3.4.1998, p. 1 , ELI: ). ( 2 ) Directive 2008/56/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 June 2008 establishing a framework for community action in the field of marine environmental policy (Marine Strategy Framework Directive) ( OJ L 164, 25.6.2008, p. 19 , ELI: ). Directives for the negotiation of an amendment to Article 1(a) of the OSPAR Convention including the water surrounding Macaronesia (Madeira and the Canary Islands) in the maritime area governed by the OSPAR Convention At the 28 th regular meeting/session of the OSPAR Commission, the Union shall support the amendment to Article 1(a) of the OSPAR Convention jointly proposed by Spain and Portugal to include the waters surrounding Macaronesia (Madeira and the Canary Islands) in the maritime area governed by the OSPAR Convention. The Union shall ensure that the amendment to Article 1(a) of the OSPAR Convention remains in line with Union legislation, including in particular Directive 2008/56/EC ( 1 ) , Council Directive 92/43/EEC ( 2 ) , Directive 2009/147/EC ( 3 ) and Regulation (EU) 2024/1991 ( 4 ) . Coherence with the EU biodiversity strategy for 2030 ( 5 ) shall also be pursued.
