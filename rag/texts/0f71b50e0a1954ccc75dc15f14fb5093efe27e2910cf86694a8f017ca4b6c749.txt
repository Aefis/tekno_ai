It shall apply from [OP please insert date: 9 months after date of entry into force ], with the exception of Article 1(2) and point 11, points 13 to 14, points 23 to 26, point 28, point 30, point 21.B.85 in point 40 and point 43 of Annex which shall apply from [OP please insert date of entry into force]. This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States. Done at Brussels, 12 March 2019.
