COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION (EU) 2025/57 of 15 January 2025 establishing the format of information to be made available by the Member States for the purposes of reporting on the implementation of Directive (EU) 2015/2193 of the European Parliament and of the Council (notified under document C(2025) 81) (Text with EEA relevance) THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, Having regard to Directive (EU) 2015/2193 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 November 2015 on the limitation of emissions of certain pollutants into the air from medium combustion plants ( 1 ) , and in particular Article 11(3), second subparagraph, thereof, Whereas: HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION: Article 1 1. For the purpose of reporting to the Commission an estimate of the total annual emissions of sulphur dioxide (SO 2 ), nitrogen oxides (NO x ) and dust pursuant to Article 11(1) of Directive (EU) 2015/2193, Member States shall use the format set out in Annex I to this Decision. 2. For the purpose of reporting to the Commission qualitative and quantitative information on the implementation of Directive (EU) 2015/2193, on any action taken to verify compliance of the operation of medium combustion plants with Directive (EU) 2015/2193 and on any enforcement action for the purposes thereof, pursuant to Article 11(1) of Directive (EU) 2015/2193, Member States shall use the format set out in Annex II to this Decision. 3. Member States shall use the electronic reporting tool referred to in Article 11(3), first subparagraph, of Directive (EU) 2015/2193 to submit the information requested in Annexes I and II to this Decision. Article 2 This Decision is addressed to the Member States. Done at Brussels, 15 January 2025. For the Commission Jessika ROSWALL Member of the Commission ( 1 ) OJ L 313, 28.11.2015, p. 1 , ELI: .
