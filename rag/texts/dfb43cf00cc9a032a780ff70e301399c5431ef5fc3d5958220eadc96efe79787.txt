COMMISSION DECISION of 23 June 2025 designating the Central Administrator of the Union Registry (C/2025/3441) THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, Having regard to Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 October 2003 establishing a system for greenhouse gas emission allowance trading within the Union and amending Council Directive 96/61/EC ( 1 ) , and in particular Article 20(1) thereof, Having regard to Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/1122 of 12 March 2019 supplementing Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the functioning of the Union Registry ( 2 ) , and in particular Article 4(1) thereof, Whereas: HAS DECIDED AS FOLLOWS: Article 1 The Director-General of the Directorate-General for Climate Action is designated as the Central Administrator of the Union Registry. Article 2 This Decision shall apply from the day of its adoption. Done at Brussels, 23 June 2025. For the Commission Wopke HOEKSTRA Member of the Commission ( 1 ) OJ L 275, 25.10.2003, p. 32 , ELI: .
