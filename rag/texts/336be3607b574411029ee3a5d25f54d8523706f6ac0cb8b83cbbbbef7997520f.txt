Avis juridique important 81/638/EEC: Commission opinion of 20 July 1981 addressed to the Government of the United Kingdom regarding the implementation of Council Directive 79/116/EEC concerning the minimum requirements for certain tankers entering or leaving Community ports and Council Directive 79/1034/EEC amending the above Directive (Only the English text is authentic) Official Journal L 231 , 15/08/1981 P. 0036 - 0036 COMMISSION OPINION of 20 July 1981 addressed to the Government of the United Kingdom regarding the implementation of Council Directive 79/116/EEC concerning the minimum requirements for certain tankers entering or leaving Community ports (1) and Council Directive 79/1034/EEC (2) amending the above Directive (Only the English text is authentic) (81/638/EEC) 1. In a letter dated 24 February 1981, the Permanent Representation of the United Kingdom to the European Communities forwarded to the Commission, under Article 3 of both Directives, a final draft Regulation designed to implement the provisions of the Directives referred to above. The letter followed an exchange of views between the United Kingdom authorities and the Commission concerning a previous draft. 2. An examination of the final draft has satisfied the Commission that the Government of the United Kingdom has fully complied with its obligations with respect to implementation of the two Directives concerned. Done at Brussels, 20 July 1981. For the Commission Georges CONTOGEORGIS Member of the Commission (1) OJ No L 33, 8.2.1979, p. 33. (2) OJ No L 315, 11.12.1979, p. 16.
