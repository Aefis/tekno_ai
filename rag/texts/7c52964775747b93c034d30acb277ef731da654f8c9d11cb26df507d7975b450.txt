COMMISSION RECOMMENDATION (EU) 2024/638 of 18 December 2023 on the consistency of Austria’s measures with the Union’s climate-neutrality objective and with ensuring progress on adaptation (Only the German text is authentic) THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, and in particular Article 292 thereof, Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2021/1119 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 30 June 2021 establishing the framework for achieving climate neutrality and amending Regulations (EC) No 401/2009 and (EU) 2018/1999 ( 1 ) , and in particular Article 7(2) thereof, Whereas: HEREBY RECOMMENDS THAT AUSTRIA TAKES ACTION TO: Consistency of national measures with the climate-neutrality objective Consistency of national measures with ensuring progress on adaptation Done at Brussels, 18 December 2023.
