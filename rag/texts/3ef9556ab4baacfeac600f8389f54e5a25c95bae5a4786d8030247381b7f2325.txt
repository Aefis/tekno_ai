Corrigendum to Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/217 of 4 October 2019 amending, for the purposes of its adaptation to technical and scientific progress, Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council on classification, labelling and packaging of substances and mixtures and correcting that Regulation ( Official Journal of the European Union L 44 of 18 February 2020 ) On page 6, in Annex II inserting row EUH 211 in Part 3 of Annex III to Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008, in the third column of the table, in the row concerning ‘RO’: ‘Atenție! Se pot forma picături respirabile periculoase la pulverizare. Nu respirați prin pulverizare sau ceață.’, ‘Atenție! La pulverizare, se pot forma picături respirabile periculoase. Nu inspirați vaporii, ceața sau aerosolii.’. On page 6, in Annex II inserting row EUH 211 in Part 3 of Annex III to Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008, in the third column of the table, in the row concerning ‘ES’: ‘¡Atención! Al rociar pueden formarse gotas respirables peligrosas. No respirar el aerosol.’, ‘¡Atención! Al rociar pueden formarse gotas respirables peligrosas. No respirar el aerosol o la niebla.’.
